## Status vocabulary

- `CONFIRMED` — reproduced against the frozen baseline.
- `REMEDIATED` — a fix exists, but the original reproduction has not yet been re-run.
- `CLOSED` — identical reproduction now passes after remediation.
- `NOT A FINDING` — inspected and no vulnerability/defect was demonstrated.

No severity label is assigned without a concrete demonstrated impact.
